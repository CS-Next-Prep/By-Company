> Coding interview questions, organised by company.

ByCompany is a focused reference for technical interview preparation. Choose a company, review the questions associated with it, and spend your time preparing instead of searching through scattered lists.

[Visit ByCompany →](https://by-company.vercel.app)

## What you can do

- Browse companies from A to Z
- Search across companies and question titles
- Review difficulty, topic tags, and frequency where available
- Compare question lists from two companies
- Open the original LeetCode page for any question

## Built for focused preparation

Whether you are preparing for one company or comparing a shortlist, ByCompany keeps the process straightforward: find the company, scan the questions, and start practising.

There are no accounts, subscriptions, or distractions—just a practical directory for interview preparation.

## A note on question content

ByCompany lists problem titles, difficulty, tags, frequency, and links to the original LeetCode pages. It does not reproduce LeetCode problem statements.
